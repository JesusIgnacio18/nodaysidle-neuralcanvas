# NeuralCanvas - How to Use

Welcome to NeuralCanvas! This app transforms your hand-drawn sketches into polished UI wireframes. No design experience needed.

---

## Getting Started

When you first open NeuralCanvas, you'll see a quick 3-step tutorial. Follow along to learn the basics, or skip it if you're eager to dive in.

---

## The Basics

### 1. Create a New Project

- Click **File → New Project** (or press **⌘N**)
- Give your project a name
- Your project is saved automatically as you work

### 2. Draw Your Sketch

Use your mouse or trackpad to draw on the canvas:

- **Click and drag** to draw shapes
- Draw rough rectangles, circles, or lines — they don't need to be perfect!
- NeuralCanvas recognizes your sketches and converts them to clean shapes

**Drawing Tips:**
- Draw a rectangle for buttons, cards, or containers
- Draw circles for avatars or icons
- Draw lines for dividers or navigation bars
- Draw text-like scribbles for text placeholders

### 3. Watch the Magic

As you draw, NeuralCanvas automatically:
- Recognizes what you're trying to draw
- Converts rough sketches into clean geometric shapes
- Arranges elements into a proper wireframe layout

---

## View Modes

Switch between different views using the toolbar or keyboard shortcuts:

| Mode | Shortcut | Description |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **Sketch** | **⌘1** | See your original hand-drawn sketches |
| **Wireframe** | **⌘2** | See the clean, generated wireframe |
| **Split** | **⌘3** | See both side-by-side |

---

## Style Mirror (Optional)

Want your wireframe to match an existing design? Use Style Mirror:

1. Click **Style → Import Style from Image** (or press **⌘I**)
2. Select a screenshot of a design you like
3. NeuralCanvas extracts the colors, fonts, and spacing
4. Your wireframe updates to match that style

---

## Undo & Redo

Made a mistake? No problem:

- **Undo:** Press **⌘Z** or click **Edit → Undo**
- **Redo:** Press **⇧⌘Z** or click **Edit → Redo**

---

## Exporting Your Work

When you're happy with your wireframe, export it:

1. Click **File → Export** (or press **⌘E**)
2. Choose your format:
   - **SVG** — Best for web developers (scalable, editable)
   - **PDF** — Best for sharing and printing
   - **PNG** — Best for quick previews and presentations
   - **JPEG** — Best for smaller file sizes
3. Choose where to save
4. Done!

**Export Tips:**
- Use **2x scale** for crisp images on high-resolution displays
- Check **"Include background"** if you want a white background
- Use **"Crop to content"** to remove extra whitespace

## Managing Projects

### Open an Existing Project
- Click **File → Open** (or press **⌘O**)
- Select your project from the list

### Duplicate a Project
- Right-click the project in the sidebar
- Select **Duplicate**

### Delete a Project
- Right-click the project in the sidebar
- Select **Delete**
- Confirm deletion (this cannot be undone)

---

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Draw one shape at a time** — NeuralCanvas works best when it can recognize individual elements

2. **Use simple shapes** — Rectangles, circles, and lines are recognized most reliably

3. **Leave space between elements** — This helps the app understand your layout

4. **Start rough, refine later** — Your first sketch doesn't need to be perfect; you can always adjust

5. **Use Style Mirror** — Importing a style from an existing design gives your wireframes a professional look instantly
